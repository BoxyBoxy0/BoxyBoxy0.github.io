How Online Lessons Work

1. What students need

• A laptop or desktop computer (Windows, Mac, or Chromebook)
• A stable internet connection
• Zoom (free – no paid account required)

Tablets and phones are not suitable for coding lessons.

2. Before the lesson

After booking, I’ll send a short email with everything needed to get started. Students simply:
• Join the Zoom call using the provided link
• Open a browser-based Python editor (no downloads required)

3. During the lesson

Lessons are 1:1 and interactive. Students share their screen while coding, and I guide them step-by-step through examples and problems.

Sessions focus on understanding and confidence — not rushing through content.

4. After the lesson

By the end of each session, students know exactly what they’ve learned and what comes next. Optional small practice tasks can be provided on request.
